## The entry

> Stay for the last ten minutes of the lecture on 14 March — the woman in the red coat in row three is the one you marry.
> Leave early and you spend four years, eleven first dates, and one very bad haircut trying to find her again.

One specific moment, one literal price. Stay and you meet the woman you marry; leave early and you pay for it with four years, eleven first dates, and one very bad haircut.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the two-line entry to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 228 characters, exactly two lines, no trailing newline). Python standard library only — no network, no dependencies.
